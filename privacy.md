This notice covers this website. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from the app's page. Deutsche Fassung unten.

## Who is responsible

The developer named in the [Impressum](impressum), reachable at support@setjot.app.

## Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. When you open a page, GitHub receives your IP address, the page you asked for, the date and time, and technical details of your browser, and may keep them in server logs to run and secure the service. GitHub may process this data in the United States, under the safeguards in its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in offering this website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The developer does not receive these logs.

## Nothing else

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising. Fonts and images are served from this website, so your browser makes no requests to other companies. When an app opens one of its pages here, the same applies.

## Email

If you write to the developer, your address and message are used to reply and kept only as long as needed. The legal basis is the developer's legitimate interest in answering you.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. For server logs, contact GitHub. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.
