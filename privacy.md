# SetJot Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

## 1. In short

SetJot has no account, no analytics and no tracking by the developer. What you log stays on your device. The developer does not receive it and cannot see it. SetJot shows a small banner ad now and then, provided by Google AdMob; it never gets your workout data.

## 2. What is stored on your device

Your sessions, sets, routines, folders and notes. Body measurements you enter, such as weight, body fat, waist and height. Profile details you choose to enter, such as birthday and gender. Your settings, such as unit, language, theme and rest timer.

They are kept in a database in the app’s private storage on your phone.

## 3. What the developer collects

Nothing. SetJot has no sign-in and no developer servers, and the developer runs no analytics, crash reporting or tracking. The one third-party service that handles data is Google AdMob, which shows the ads; see Ads.

## 4. Ads

After you have logged sets on several days, SetJot shows a small banner ad on the Stats screen, at most once a day and for about 20 seconds. Ads come from Google AdMob. To show an ad, Google’s software in the app sends your device’s IP address, device and app details and, where your device allows it, its advertising ID to Google, which may use them to choose and measure ads under Google’s privacy policy. SetJot sends Google none of your training, body or health data.

Where the law requires it, such as in the EEA and UK, SetJot first shows a consent form from Google and loads ads only if you allow it. SetJot does not ask permission to track you across other apps, so on iPhone the advertising ID is not shared. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device’s privacy settings. If the form applies to you, you can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings, About, Ad privacy choices.

Where consent is required, ads are shown on the basis of your consent; elsewhere, on the basis of the developer’s legitimate interest in funding the app through advertising. Google may process this data in other countries, including the United States, under its own safeguards.

## 5. Apple Health and Health Connect

This is optional and only starts after you allow it. SetJot writes your finished workouts and the weight, body fat, height and (on Apple Health) waist you log. It never reads health data from Apple Health or Health Connect. You can change or withdraw access at any time in your device’s settings.

Once written, the data lives in that health app under its own privacy rules. SetJot does not use it for advertising or share it with anyone. While syncing is on, deleting a session or body entry in SetJot also asks the health app to remove the matching entry SetJot wrote.

## 6. Notifications and lock screen

Rest alerts are optional notifications that your phone schedules by itself. They need no push server and no account. On iPhone, a Live Activity can show your current workout on the lock screen; it is created on the device too. By default the screen stays awake during a session; you can turn this off in Settings. None of this sends data anywhere.

## 7. Internet use

SetJot works offline. It uses the internet for two things. The first is the exercise video: it plays through YouTube’s embedded player when you open it, so YouTube (Google) receives your device’s IP address and technical details, and may store cookies or identifiers under its own privacy policy. SetJot sends YouTube none of your training data. “Open in YouTube” opens the video in the YouTube app or your browser. The second is the ad banner, described under Ads.

Exercise pictures and guides are part of the app. SetJot makes no other connections of its own.

## 8. Exports and sharing

You can export your log as CSV or JSON and import a backup. The file is created on your device and handed to the share sheet, so where it goes is your choice, and the developer receives nothing. Exports contain personal data, so share them with care.

## 9. Device backups

Your phone’s own backup service, such as iCloud or Google backup, may include SetJot’s data, depending on your device settings. That is between you and the provider.

## 10. If you email the developer

If you write through Contact, the developer receives your email address and your message. They are used to reply and to improve the app, and kept only as long as needed. Email is handled by your provider and the developer’s. Please do not include health details.

## 11. Your choices and rights

Your data is on your device, so you control it: view, edit, export or delete it in the app, or remove everything by deleting the app. Exports, entries in Apple Health or Health Connect, and device backups are separate copies and are deleted where they are kept.

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to access or erase on the developer’s side. If you have rights under privacy law where you live, these tools and the Contact option are how you use them. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority where you live.

## 12. Children

SetJot is not aimed at children and is not meant for anyone under 16. The developer collects no personal information from anyone. Parents and guardians should help young people train safely.

## 13. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
